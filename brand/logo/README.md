## Brand logo "Valett." (from the App Design Showcase)

Bold grotesk "Valett." with the full stop, as on the showcase's cover. The showcase only holds it as a picture,
so it is rebuilt here as vectors: Inter Tight, weight 700, letter spacing -0.02em, with the font's own kerning
(the "a" tucks under the "V"). Matched by overlaying it on the showcase render. Colours: ink #0A0A0A (primary,
on white), white on black, or gold #D4AF66 on black (brand guidelines). Clear space: at least 20 px around it;
minimum 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
