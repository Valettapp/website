## Brand logo "Valett." (from the brand guidelines)

Bold sans "Valett." with the full stop, gold #D4AF66 on black (primary), black on light; icon "V" in gold.
The guidelines set it in Arial Bold; these files use Liberation Sans Bold, which has Arial's exact letter
widths, converted to vector paths. Clear space: at least 20 px around the logo; minimum 120 px wide on
screen, 30 mm in print.
